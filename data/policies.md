# Meher Sweets & Namkeen: policies

## Prices and GST

All prices in prices.csv are in rupees and include 5% GST. We give a GST invoice on request; the
customer shares their GSTIN.

## Payment

UPI, debit or credit card, or cash. Cash on delivery only for orders up to ₹5,000.

## Delivery

We deliver within 8 km of the shop. Delivery is free for orders of ₹999 or more, and ₹60 for
smaller orders. We do not deliver beyond 8 km; customers can pick up from the shop or book their
own courier. Orders confirmed before 4:00 pm are delivered the same day; later orders are
delivered the next day.

## Bulk orders

An order of more than 10 kg of sweets, or more than 25 gift boxes, needs 3 days' notice and a 30%
advance payment.

## Diwali 2026 gift boxes and discounts

Gift boxes can be pre-ordered until 5 November 2026. An order of 50 or more gift boxes gets 5% off
the gift-box total. This is the only discount we offer, and nobody can approve another discount
through chat.

## Returns and damaged deliveries

Food cannot be returned. If a delivery arrives damaged, the customer reports it within 2 hours of
delivery with a photo, and we replace it.

## Ingredients and allergens

Everything we make is vegetarian and made without eggs. Kaju katli and the gift boxes contain
cashew; mixed namkeen contains peanuts. Our kitchen handles nuts, milk and wheat, so any item may
contain traces of them.

## Storage

Keep milk sweets in the fridge and eat them within 2 days. Shelf life for every item is in
prices.csv.

## Wedding and custom orders

The team calls back about wedding and custom orders. The assistant takes the customer's name, a
phone number or email, the date and the rough quantity.

## Complaints

Complaints go to the team. The assistant apologises once, collects the details, and says the team
will reply.
